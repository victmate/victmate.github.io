Our first book

FOCUS & START

A Simple Productivity Planner for Distracted Minds

ADHD-friendly Low-friction Undated

For distracted days, overloaded weeks, and tasks you know you need to do — but somehow never start. FOCUS & START is VICTMATE's first book. It helps you get things out of your head, choose what matters, make the next step smaller, and actually begin.

It's an undated planner, so there are no wasted pages and no guilt over missed days: skip a day, a week, a month — then simply pick up where you left off and restart.

Inside the planner

Everything you need. Nothing that slows you down.

Seven simple tools, designed to take seconds — so planning never becomes another task on the list.

  • Simple weekly planning — see the whole week on one calm page.
  • One clear daily priority — one thing that makes today count.
  • Next-action breakdown — big tasks, cut down to a first step you can start.
  • Distraction parking — a place to park stray thoughts until later.
  • Flexible time anchors — structure your day without a rigid schedule.
  • Quick weekly resets — close the week and set up the next in minutes.
  • Easy restart after missed days — no catching up, no guilt. Just begin again.
